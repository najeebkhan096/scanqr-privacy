ScanQR Privacy Policy

Applies to the ScanQR – QR & Barcode Scanner app (Android, iOS and web) · Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: ScanQR works on your device. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal information. Your scan history, favorites and settings stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. We (the developer of ScanQR) do not receive any personal or sensitive data from the app: no name, email address, account, device identifier, advertising ID, location, contacts, photos or analytics. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device (in the browser's storage on the web). It is never sent to us:

DataPurpose
Content of the codes you scan, the code format, type and timeYour scan history
Codes you create with the generator, if you choose to save themYour history and favorites
Favorites (a flag on a saved item)Quick access to important items
Theme preference (light, dark or system)Appearance

This data is held in the app's private storage and protected by your device's own security (for example the screen lock and Android/iOS app sandboxing). The app does not add its own encryption on top of that. Scanned codes can contain sensitive text (such as Wi-Fi passwords), so consider this when sharing or exporting your history. On Android, cloud backup is switched off for the app, so this data is not copied to your Google account by backup.

3. Camera

ScanQR asks for permission to use the camera only to read QR codes and barcodes while the scanner screen is open. Camera images are analysed on your device and are not stored, recorded or uploaded. The camera is released when you leave the scanner or the app moves to the background. You can refuse or withdraw camera permission at any time in your device or browser settings; you can still type or paste a code manually.

4. Permissions

  • Android: camera only. The released Android app does not request the internet permission.
  • iOS: camera only.
  • Web: camera, requested by your browser only after you choose to start scanning.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, because we do not have it. The following happens only when you choose it:

  • Opening a link, calling, emailing, texting or viewing a location hands the item to your browser, phone, email or map app (or, on web and iOS, the OpenStreetMap website for coordinates). ScanQR never does this automatically, and shows the destination of a link before you confirm. Those apps and websites have their own privacy practices.
  • Sharing, copying or exporting (the share sheet, clipboard, CSV file or QR image) sends the content to the app or place you pick.

6. Components used by the app

  • Barcode recognition. On Android the app includes Google's ML Kit barcode scanner, bundled in the app so recognition runs on your device. Without an internet permission, the Android app cannot send data over the network. On the web, recognition uses your browser's built-in barcode detector or an open-source decoder (ZXing, as WebAssembly) served with the app.
  • Web hosting. The web version is delivered as static files. If a scanned text contains characters that the app's bundled font lacks (for example some non-Latin scripts or emoji), your browser may download a font file for those characters; the text itself is not sent.
  • Google Play / App Store. If you install from a store, the store may collect its own information about installs and crashes under its own policy. We do not receive personal data from it.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete a single scan, clear your history or favorites inside the app (History and Settings), or remove everything by uninstalling the app or, on the web, clearing the site's data in your browser. Because we hold no copy, we cannot retrieve or delete your data for you.

8. Children

ScanQR is a general-purpose utility, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server-side copy that could be exposed. Keep your device updated and locked to protect what is stored on it. We make no claim of any security certification.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect in a release.
